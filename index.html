How this version works
This version adds the systems you asked for.
  1. Tap a producer to select it. Tap it again to spawn (or collect) an item into any free tile around it.
  2. Some producers run on Energy (tap to spend ⚡). Others are Auto — they refill on a timer and glow green with a ✓ when ready to collect for free.
  3. Merge identical items. Every merge gives a little XP.
  4. Completing an Alien Buyer's contract gives the biggest XP reward, plus coins, fuel and research.
  5. Leveling up refills your Energy to full and opens more board space.
  6. Watch for meteor crashes ☄️ — they drop a rare Meteor Shard straight onto your board.
  7. Stuck? Tap 💡 Hint to see a mergeable pair highlighted — or wait, it'll flash automatically if you're idle.
  8. Open Guide (📖) to see every merge chain and what each producer makes.
  9. Open Galaxy, choose a planet, and spend Fuel to travel. Each planet has its own board.
  10. Later use Research to combine items from different planets.
