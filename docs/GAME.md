# Galaxy Adventure: what is in the game

## The loop
Tap **producers** (1 energy a tap) for items → drag two of the same together to **merge** them one step up their chain → fill **contracts** from the aliens above the board for coins, XP and gifts → finish **chapters** in the Mission Log to restore the world, unlock new producers and move the story on. Energy refills over time (and from gifts, the wheel, ads, purchases).

The rocket is rebuilt from four parts found in Dr. Zonk's wreck, fuelled with ore from meteor craters (a meteor falls every 20–35 minutes), and flies between worlds (a new world costs 5 fuel, flying back is free).

## Catalogue
1000 items in 155 chains, 129 producers. Every merge step is the same thing, bigger or more of it. Everything is space and alien: no Earth plants, animals, food or objects.

## Systems
- **Contracts**: never the same producer twice in a row; a milestone chest every few contracts; the Star Freighter docks now and then with a bigger order.
- **Mission Log**: chapters, Medals (achievements), Cadet Training (tasks), Relic Vault (forever perks), the weekly Star Challenge and the Daily Log.
- **Shop (Star Bazaar, level 4)**: a free daily gift, energy, sticker packs, chests, boosters, gems and coins, permanent upgrades.
- **Dr. Zonk's Lab** (built from a chapter): Fusion, Recycler (spares → Science), Time Boost, Incubator (pet eggs), Condenser, Upgrades.
- **Pets**: one at a time, hard to hatch, each with its own perk.
- **Meadow Heart / Bloom**: finishing a chain for the first time gives Bloom essence; feed the world's Heart to wake the planet.
- **Star Chart**: craters give Star Scrap → fused into Star Cores → light constellations for perks and gifts (10 constellations, 6 to 60 cores each).
- **Star Album**: seasonal sticker album (10 sets × 9), packs from levels, chapters, chests and contracts; dust swap; a badge for finishing.
- **Star Expeditions (level 5)**: a separate event board every month with its own producer, two chains, visitors' requests, a 10-step points track, a discovery log and four medals. Six expeditions rotate (October: Nebula Spooks).
- **Timed events** on the main board: Meteor Shower, Bloom Festival, Comet Carnival.
- **Side games**: lucky wheel, pairs, bingo, digging, brewing, market.
- **Storage**: stash items and producers off the board and place them back.
- **Profile (tap the level badge)**: name, stats, album, share card.

## Monetisation
Rewarded videos only (the player chooses: energy, double rewards, skips); in-app purchases for gems, coins, energy, a starter pack and an ad-free pass. No forced ads.

## Look and feel
Art: painted in ChatGPT in one style (chunky, glossy, bright, readable on a small tile). UI: "Pocket Buddy": light aqua holo panels projected by a floating companion robot (src/themes/), orange primary buttons, aqua glass board tiles, Lilita One titles, Fredoka body text. The older Space Hologram look stays available in Settings.
