Galaxy Adventure – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Galaxy Adventure ("the game") is made by Artur Solak ("we"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: artursolakdev@gmail.com.

What we do not collect

The game has no accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress is saved on your own device.

What is collected, and by whom

ServiceWhatWhy
Google Firebase AnalyticsHow the game is used (for example levels reached, chapters finished, screens opened), device model, operating system, app version, approximate country (from the IP address), an app instance identifier and, where allowed, the advertising IDTo understand which parts of the game work and to improve it
Google Firebase CrashlyticsCrash reports and error details, device state at the time of a crash, the last game events before itTo find and fix bugs
Google AdMobAdvertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactionsTo show the optional reward videos you choose to watch, measure them and prevent fraud
Google Play Billing and RevenueCatPurchase history and an anonymous purchase identifier (we never see your card or payment details)To deliver what you buy and restore purchases

These providers process data under their own privacy policies: Google, RevenueCat. We do not sell your personal information.

Ads and consent

Ads in the game are only shown when you choose to watch a video for a reward. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your energy is full). They are created on your phone and are not sent through a server.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will delete what we can.

Keeping and deleting data

Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months; crash reports for 90 days. Uninstalling the game deletes your saved progress from the device. To ask for deletion of analytics data linked to your device, or for any question about your data, email artursolakdev@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data and to complain to your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
