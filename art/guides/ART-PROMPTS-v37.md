## Brand (the game is now called **Gloop Galaxy**)

**55. logo** (transparent, 1536×1024): the game logo, text "Gloop Galaxy" on two lines.
- "Gloop" in chunky, bubbly lime-green letters that look like glossy goo, with a drip under the "p".
- "Galaxy" in golden-orange letters with tiny stars sparkling inside them.
- A thick dark-purple outline, a thin white inner rim, and a soft drop shadow.
- A small ringed planet behind the "G", and Gloop the mascot peeking over the top edge of the "oo".
- No other text.

**56. app_icon** (square, 1024×1024, full bleed, no transparency, no text):
- Gloop the mascot climbing out of his glowing potion flask, waving.
- Behind him a deep purple-blue galaxy with a lime-green glow and a few stars.
- Big and readable at 48 px. No border and no rounded corners (the phone adds those).

**57. loading_screen** (portrait, 1024×1792, no text):
- A cosy alien planet seen from just above, under a starry nebula sky.
- Captain Bob (lime-green alien, orange space-scout jacket, goggles) stands in the lower left, waving with a tiny rocket model.
- Gloop in his flask sits in the lower right.
- The Freighter UFO floats top right; a ringed planet glows in the sky.
- Keep the top third fairly calm for the logo.

**58. feature_graphic** (wide, 1024×500, no text): the same scene for the Play Store banner, with Bob and Gloop on the right, open sky on the left.
