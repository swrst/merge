## SETUP (send once, attach STYLE-REFERENCE.png)

We're making art for my mobile merge game "Merge Rocket": Travel Town style, space and alien theme, a cosy merge board on alien planets. Keep EXACTLY the style of the attached reference:
- chunky, rounded, toy-like objects
- soft glossy painterly semi-3D, bright saturated colours
- warm key light from the upper left with one soft white highlight
- shadows are richer versions of the base colour, never grey or black
- thin darker warm-brown outline, small soft contact shadow
- three-quarter view from about 30° above

The world is "Earth, but alien". Familiar things get a friendly space twist: a soft glow, a little extra eye, antennae, a ring like Saturn, star freckles, a saucer or rocket shape. Never scary, never gross.

Rules for SHEETS:
- exactly the grid I give
- every object centred in its own cell with WIDE empty gaps; nothing touches a neighbour, including glows and sparkles
- plain pure-white background, no grid lines, no text, no labels
- read left→right, then the next row; cells marked EMPTY stay empty
- in a chain each step is clearly bigger, richer and more special than the last: step 1 is tiny and plain, the last is a grand crown piece with sparkle
- square 2048×2048; every object must read as a 60 px icon

Rules for UI pieces: flat front-on view (no perspective), centred, transparent or pure-white background, no text on them unless I ask.

Answer each of my next messages with exactly ONE image.
