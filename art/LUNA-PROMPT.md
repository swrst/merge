We're continuing the art for my mobile merge game "Merge Rocket" (Travel Town style). This batch is World 2, **Crater Camp**: a friendly cartoon moon.
Keep EXACTLY the style of the attached references (our Earth items and the Moon Geyser / Glow Pod producers): one chunky, rounded, toy-like object per cell, soft glossy painterly semi-3D, bright saturated colours, warm key light from the upper left with one soft white highlight, shaded side a deeper richer version of the base colour (never grey/black), thin darker warm-brown edge line, small soft contact shadow, three-quarter view from ~30° above.
Crater Camp palette: lilac, pale silver, slate blue, soft violet, with glowing cyan accents. Cosy and inviting, never dark or scary.

Rules for every SHEET:
- A grid exactly as specified (columns × rows), every object centred in its own cell with **wide empty gaps** between cells — nothing touches or overlaps a neighbour.
- Plain flat **pure white** background (or transparent), no grid lines, no frames, no cards, no shadows between cells except each object's own small contact shadow.
- Order is reading order: left→right, then next row. Cells marked EMPTY stay completely empty.
- Inside a chain, each step must look clearly bigger, richer and more special than the previous one. Step 1 is tiny and plain; the last step is a grand "crown" piece with a little sparkle.
- No text, letters, numbers or labels anywhere. Square image, 2048×2048 if you can.
- Every object must still read as a 60-pixel icon: bold simple silhouette.

Attach as style reference: `src/sprites/producers/geyser.png`, `src/sprites/producers/glowpod.png`, and 2–3 Earth items (e.g. `src/sprites/items/twig.png`, `log.png`, `treehouse.png`).
